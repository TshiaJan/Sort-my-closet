# Privacy Policy for Shelf Sort: Closet Tidy

**Last updated:** September 17, 2026

Thank you for playing **Shelf Sort: Closet Tidy** ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles your information. In short: **we do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information or sensitive user data.**

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

- **No Personal Identification:** We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
- **No Location Data:** The App does not access, collect, or monitor GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell-tower location data.
- **No Device Identifiers for Tracking:** We do not track you across apps or websites using advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID).
- **No Third-Party Analytics Trackers:** The App operates self-contained and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide game progress and remember your settings, the App stores data strictly locally on your device using local storage (`localStorage`):

- **Game Progress:** Your current level number, best level achieved, completed shelves count, and in-game tidy coins.
- **User Preferences:** Sound toggle (on/off), vibration/haptic feedback toggle, reduced animation mode, theme selection (dark/light), and high-contrast letter identifiers on tiles.

This information remains entirely on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third party. You can reset or erase this data at any time by clearing the App's storage cache in your device settings.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

**Shelf Sort: Closet Tidy** is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for players of all ages. Because the App does not collect, request, or share any personal information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and applicable child privacy regulations worldwide.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services and Links

The App operates offline and independently. It does not contain links to external third-party websites or integrated external web views that collect your information.

---

## 5. Security of Your Information

Because all game data resides strictly on your local device hardware, your information is protected by your device's built-in operating system security features.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or changes in legal regulations. Any updates will be posted at this hosted URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
